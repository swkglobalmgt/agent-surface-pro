# Agent Surface Pro — Approved Brand Guidelines

## Uniform: American Flag Sleeve Patch
- **Approved:** American flag patch in **black and white only**. No gold, yellow, gray-gold, red, or blue within the flag.
- **Placement:** Right sleeve of Agent Surface Pro uniform shirts.
- **Execution:** Crisp, recognizable monochrome stars and stripes; premium, understated embroidered finish.
- **Uniform context:** Black-and-gold Agent Surface Pro branded shirts, with company identity on cap/chest. Gold accents elsewhere on the uniform are permitted, but **never within the American flag**.
- **Visual reference:** The most recently approved preview depicting a black-and-white flag on the right sleeve supersedes the earlier black-and-gold flag concept.

## Existing Website Visual Direction
- Premium museum-white visual aesthetic (not a literal museum setting).
- Oversized dimensional black/gunmetal AGENT typography; SURFACE PRO secondary; PRO in gold.
- Modern, cinematic commercial exterior pressure-washing presentation; realistic water/runoff and clear cleaning line.
- Worker styling: black branded cap, black sunglasses, mustache and chin goatee, short natural hair, no jewelry, athletic but not exaggerated, black-and-gold uniform.
- Navigation: Home, Services, Industries, Projects, About, Contact; Request a Quote CTA.
- Client Access near the bottom of the homepage, not in the main header; Client and Employee Access discreet in footer. Login functionality remains planned until implemented.
- Commercial exterior pressure washing, new construction exterior cleaning, fleet washing; limited debris removal. No hood cleaning.

## Change Control
Maintain these approved design decisions in future renders, imagery, website updates, and uniform concepts unless explicitly changed by the owner.
